Effective Date: August 15, 2026

The Digital Math E-Lab is constructed using static HTML, CSS, and Client-Side JavaScript. The developers, RIE Ajmer, and NCERT do not operate any backend databases for this specific project. We do not require account registration, and we do not actively collect personal identifiers such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers.

This data remains on your personal device and is never transmitted back to our servers. We are not liable for any data loss, or if unauthorized individuals access the data stored locally on your physical device.

4. No Protection for User-Generated Input

If you input mathematical equations, notes, or specific coordinates into the visualizer tools, do not input sensitive, personal, or confidential information. We do not encrypt or secure client-side inputs beyond standard browser capabilities, and we accept no liability if such data is exposed via browser extensions, malware on your device, or third-party interception.

As the project evolves, we may implement new features (such as analytics to track usage statistics). We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time without notice. Your continued usage signifies your acceptance of the policy as it stands at the moment of access.
